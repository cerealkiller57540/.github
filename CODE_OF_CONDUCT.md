# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the cards. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer privately through a [security report](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability) on any of the card repositories (it is private and only visible to the maintainer), and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code.
